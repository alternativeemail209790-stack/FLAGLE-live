      How to play

      Two countries are picked — a start and a target. Chat calls out countries that could form a land-border chain between them. There's no guess limit, so keep them coming.

      Colors on the globe & trail

      • Start & target country
      • On the shortest possible path — counts even before it connects to the trail
      • Accepted guess — valid and connects, but a longer route
      • Not on the shortest path and not connected to the trail
      • Outline hint — a valid next country, shape only
      • Not yet guessed

      Scoring

      • 3 points — the guess is on the shortest possible path, whether or not it's connected to the trail yet
      • 1 point — the guess is valid and connects, just not the optimal route
      • 0 points — the guess is neither on the optimal path nor connected yet
      • Both hints (outline shape, and initials) are free — use them as often as you like.
